# 3D models: power SMD parts and microSD

Simplified parametric STEP models generated by [`hardware/models3d/power.py`](../../../models3d/power.py) (CadQuery 2.8) from the manufacturers' drawings. They show the body, the height and simplified terminals for 3D checks and enclosure fitting; they are not detailed manufacturer models. Every model sits on the board surface (z = 0) at the footprint origin with offset 0 and rotation 0.

| Ref | File | Drawing | Body, mm (L × W × H above the PCB) | Attached through |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F501, F511, F521, F711, F721, F904 | `Fuse_Littelfuse_451.step` | Littelfuse 451/453 NANO2 datasheet (LCSC C3099 copy) | 6.10 × 2.69 × **2.69** (±0.25), gold end caps 1.45 | `GrowBox:Fuse_Littelfuse_451` |
| L721 | `L_Bourns_SRP1265A.step` | Bourns SRP1265A product dimensions | 13.5 × 12.5 × **6.2** (±0.3), lead-frame terminals 4.7 wide | `GrowBox:L_Bourns_SRP1265A` |
| L711 | `L_Bourns_SRP1770TA_16.9x16.9mm.step` | Bourns SRP1770TA product dimensions | 16.9 × 16.9 × **6.7** (±0.3), 17.6 across terminals | board override in `hardware/sync_models.py` (stock footprint, KiCad 10 lacks its model) |
| Q601 | `ONSemi_SO-8FL_488AA.step` | onsemi SO-8FL case 488AA | 6.0 × 5.0 × **1.0** (1.10 max) | board override (stock footprint) |
| J401 | `microSD_HRO_TF-01A.step` | HRO TF-01A drawing rev A (LCSC C91145) | shell 14.76 × 14.50 × **1.85**, contacts behind the shell, card mouth at the board edge | `GrowBox:microSD_HRO_TF-01A` |
| U902 | KiCad library `Package_SO.3dshapes/SOIC-8-1EP_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm_EP2.41x3.81mm.step` | KiCad 3D library (CC-BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad exception), not copied | 4.9 × 3.9 SOIC-8 body; only the hidden exposed pad differs from the TI DDA footprint | board override |

No EasyEDA/LCSC/JLCPCB, SnapEDA or Ultra Librarian models are used.
