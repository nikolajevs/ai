# Sources of project library data

The `EU` symbol library is generated from the explicitly numbered pin maps in `tools/generate.py`; sources are linked in `design.json` and the schematic Datasheet fields. These are schematic-stage symbols, not a manufacturer-approved CAD package.

The NMOS graphics in `NMOS_graphics.sexpr` are taken from the cached KiCad `Transistor_FET:AO3400A` symbol in the original repository's `hardware/PCB_V1/Outputs.kicad_sch`, commit `1d6b1eaf1273149b32eb1cf5c91fc69ff6a6ca09`. Physical pin numbering is separately assigned to NMOS (GDS) and NMOS_GSD; AO3422 is GSD.

Selected stock footprints were retrieved on 20 October 2026 from the official [KiCad footprint library](https://github.com/KiCad/kicad-footprints). Only the selected footprints are bundled, with their library names retained in `fp-lib-table`. The upstream license is included alongside this file. `Capacitor_THT:CP_Radial_D8.0mm_P5.00mm` was added on 21 October 2026 from upstream commit `7ebfa6b23cc292a56f751b7b5f4a0e12eeef69dd` (replacing the 3.5 mm variant); only its 3D model path was changed to the local `Panasonic_EEUFR1J101LB_D8_L15_P5.step`.

`EU_Parts:ESP32-WROOM-32E_NoVias` and `EU_Parts:Vishay_TO-277A_D_K1_A2` are copied without geometry changes from `hardware/PCB_V1/libraries/GrowBox.pretty` at the same source commit. They describe the same module package and rectifier already used in PCB_V1. On 21 October their 3D filenames were changed to the bundled models listed below; physical package qualification remains open.

All assigned footprints remain subject to datasheet/physical qualification in the new board. All formerly missing lands now have explicit drawing-based assignments in `../footprint_assignments.json`. This is not physical-fit or assembly qualification; other package-only assignments still require their own datasheet review.

## Bundled STEP models, 21 October 2026

`EU.3dshapes` contains 32 STEP files. The authoritative per-file origin, URL, licence and SHA-256 are in `../models3d.json`. Thirty-one are attached to all 185 fitted components; the heatsink envelope is deliberately unattached.

Sixteen models are original simplified CadQuery geometry, licensed under MIT (`EU.3dshapes/LICENSE-own-models.txt`), generated by `../tools/make_models3d.py` with CadQuery 2.7.0. No commercial CAD-library geometry was used. Coordinate frame: mm, STEP X = footprint X, STEP Y = negative footprint Y, Z = 0 at the PCB component face; KiCad applies bottom-side transforms. Body heights are maximum where the source specifies a maximum; capacitor dimensions are nominal with an assumed 0.15 mm standoff. Threading, springs, cavities, pin bends and fillets without explicit dimensions are simplified and are not manufacturing data.
